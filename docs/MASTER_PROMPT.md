SECTION 5: AI RECRUITMENT SYSTEM — END-TO-END
5.1 Complete Hiring Pipeline
AI handles 90% of the recruitment process autonomously. HR team only makes the final Go/No-Go decision.

5.1.1 Job Requisition Phase
1.HR Manager submits new hire request with department, role, budget, timeline
2.AI generates optimized Job Description from role template + market data
3.AI sets AI-matching criteria: Required skills, experience bands, salary range
4.Job auto-posted to: Company website, LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Shine, WorkIndia, WhatsApp status
5.AI activates passive talent pool search from existing database

5.1.2 Application Processing Phase
6.Candidate applies via any channel
7.AI parses resume: Extracts name, email, phone, education, experience, skills, certifications
8.AI scores candidate: 0-100 based on JD match (40%), experience (30%), skills (20%), education (10%)
9.AI auto-categorizes: Hot (80+), Warm (60-79), Cold (40-59), Reject (<40)
10.Duplicate detection: AI identifies same candidate applied multiple times
11.AI talent pool match: Check if candidate fits any other open role better

5.1.3 AI Screening Phase
12.Hot & Warm candidates receive automated WhatsApp message with screening link
13.AI conducts async voice screening (10 questions, 2 min each)
14.NLP analyzes: Communication clarity, technical accuracy, confidence level
15.AI generates screening report with score and recommendation
16.HR receives ranked shortlist with AI rationale for each candidate

5.1.4 Interview Scheduling Phase
17.AI checks interviewer calendars via Google Calendar / Outlook integration
18.Candidate receives 3 time slot options via WhatsApp + Email + SMS
19.Candidate selects slot; AI auto-creates meeting (Google Meet / Zoom / Teams)
20.AI sends confirmation to all parties with meeting link, JD, candidate profile
21.AI sends reminders: 24 hours before, 2 hours before, 15 minutes before
22.No-show detection: If candidate doesn't join in 10 min, AI auto-reschedules

5.1.5 AI Interview Analysis Phase
23.AI joins interview as silent observer (with candidate consent)
24.Real-time transcription of entire interview
25.AI analyzes: Answer quality, keyword match, confidence score, communication
26.Emotion detection (beta): Nervousness, enthusiasm, evasiveness
27.Post-interview: AI generates interview scorecard for interviewer
28.Interviewer submits feedback; AI cross-validates with AI observations

5.1.6 Selection & Rejection Phase
Decision	AI Action	Communication	Timeline
SELECTED	Generate offer letter template	WhatsApp + Email: Congratulations + next steps	Within 2 hours
SELECTED	Trigger onboarding workflow	Send onboarding form link	Same day
SELECTED	Update HR dashboard	Notify HR Manager + Dept Head	Real-time
REJECTED	Generate rejection email	Professional rejection with feedback	Within 24 hours
REJECTED	Add to talent pool	Tag with skills, role fit, reject reason	Automated
