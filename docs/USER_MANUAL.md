# AKUL DRAVIN HRMS AI - User Manual

Welcome to the AKUL DRAVIN HRMS! This guide will help you navigate your new workspace and utilize the power of our Sovereign AI OS.

## 1. Getting Started

### Login & Dashboard
- Navigate to the platform's login page and enter your credentials.
- Upon logging in, you will be directed to your role-specific dashboard.
- The platform utilizes a stunning 3D Glassmorphism interface. Use the sidebar to navigate between your authorized modules (HR, Finance, AI Hub, etc.).

## 2. Employee Self Service (ESS)

Every employee has access to their own workspace to manage day-to-day activities:
- **Attendance**: Log your check-ins and check-outs. Depending on your organization's settings, this may include biometric or Geo-attendance tracking.
- **Leave Management**: Apply for leaves, view holiday calendars, and check your leave balances.
- **Documents**: Securely access your offer letters, payslips, and tax documents. All documents are cryptographically verified.
- **Helpdesk**: Raise tickets for IT, HR, or Facility issues directly from your portal.

## 3. Human Resources (HR) Module

If you are an HR manager, you have elevated access to the employee lifecycle:
- **Recruitment (ATS)**: Create job postings, review applications, and move candidates through the pipeline.
- **Onboarding**: Send automated onboarding sequences, collect documents, and assign assets to new hires.
- **Performance**: Set OKRs/KPIs, conduct 360-degree reviews, and track employee growth.
- **Communications**: Send organization-wide announcements and manage the internal Job Board.

## 4. Finance & Payroll

For Accountants and Finance managers:
- **Payroll Execution**: Run weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly payrolls. The system ensures deterministic zero-sum balancing, meaning every cent is accounted for.
- **Expenses**: Approve or reject employee expense claims with AI-assisted receipt scanning.
- **Tax & Compliance**: Generate statutory reports and ensure compliance with local tax laws.

## 5. The AI Assistant

Your workspace includes an integrated AI Assistant powered by the Node 8 Engine.
- **Querying**: You can ask the assistant natural language questions (e.g., "How many leaves do I have left?" or "Show me the payroll summary for Q2").
- **Insights**: The AI proactively surfaces anomalies and suggestions on your dashboard to help you make better decisions.
